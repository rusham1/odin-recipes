COFFEE MAKING


Description

To make desi-style coffee, start by boiling water with sugar in a saucepan. Add instant coffee powder and stir well. Then add milk and bring the mixture to a boil again. For a richer flavor, you can froth the coffee by pouring it back and forth between cups. Serve hot for a cozy, aromatic drink.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1–2 teaspoons instant coffee powder
  • 1–2 teaspoons sugar (adjust to taste)

Steps

  1. Boil Water:
    • In a saucepan, add 1/2 cup of water and bring it to a boil.
  2. Add Sugar and Coffee:
    • Add 1–2 teaspoons of sugar and 1–2 teaspoons of instant coffee powder to the boiling water.
    • Stir until fully dissolved.
  3. Add Milk:
    • Add 1 cup of milk to the mixture.
    • Let it boil again and simmer for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Froth the Coffee (Optional):
    • For extra froth, pour the coffee back and forth between two cups carefully.
  5. Serve:
    • Pour into a cup and serve hot. Enjoy!
