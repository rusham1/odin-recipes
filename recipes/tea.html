TEA MAKING


Description

To make milk tea, start by boiling water and steeping black tea leaves (or a tea bag) for 3–5 minutes until the flavor is rich. Remove the tea leaves or bag, then add sugar to taste while the tea is still hot. Pour in warm or cold milk (dairy or non-dairy) and stir well to blend. For iced milk tea, let it cool before adding ice. Optional toppings like tapioca pearls (boba) or flavored syrups can be added for variety.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 1–2 teaspoons black tea leaves (or 1 black tea bag)
  • 1/2 cup milk (dairy or non-dairy like oat, almond, or soy)
  • 1–2 teaspoons sugar (adjust to taste)

Steps

  1. Boil Water:
    • Pour 1 cup of water into a saucepan and bring it to a boil over medium heat.
  2. Add Tea Leaves:
    • Add 1–2 teaspoons of strong black tea leaves (like Assam tea).
    • Let it boil for 2–3 minutes to release the flavor.
  3. Add Spices (Optional but Traditional):
    • Add crushed cardamom pods, a small piece of ginger, cloves, or cinnamon—based on your preference—for a classic masala chai twist.
  4. Add Milk:
    • Add 1/2 to 3/4 cup of milk (adjust for strength and creaminess).
    • Let it boil again and simmer for 2–3 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. Add Sugar:
    • Add 1–2 teaspoons of sugar (or more, depending on your taste) and stir well until it dissolves.
  6. Simmer:
    • Allow the tea to simmer for another 2–3 minutes to blend the flavors and deepen the color.
  7. Strain and Serve: :
    • Strain the tea into cups using a tea strainer. Serve hot.
